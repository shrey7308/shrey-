A NOTE TO MY FUTURE SELF
Keep asking why. Keep testing what-if.

My name is Shrey Patel, and I’m majoring in Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning in the Pennoni Honors College. I became interested in the field in high school, when I took computer science classes. AI was beginning to grow rapidly, and I was fascinated by how it was appearing across so many fields. When I learned that Drexel had just introduced an AI/ML major that year, it felt like the right path for me. One moment that made AI feel real was using Face ID on my phone. It’s a small part of my daily routine, but recognizing a face in a second involves a lot behind the scenes. That made me want to understand how it works.

I also have concerns about AI, especially its environmental impact. Data centers use large amounts of water to cool the systems that run AI, and I worry that this use will continue to grow without enough public attention. In this course, I’m especially interested in how search and adversarial reasoning help AI behave intelligently. I want to understand the logic behind how AI makes decisions, not just see the results. That’s why I chose this major: I want to know how AI works, not just how to use it. Outside of school, I’m a big football fan, and I root for the Cleveland Browns.
